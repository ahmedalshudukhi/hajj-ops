Cardiac Arrest — Adult BLS/ALS

Source: AHA 2020 + local protocols. Open /code for run timer.
1
Verify arrest: Pulse check ≤10s. If absent → begin CPR immediately.
2
CPR: 30:2 if no advanced airway, continuous if intubated. Rate 100-120/min, depth 5-6cm. Minimize interruptions.
3
Defibrillator pads on: Analyze rhythm.
4
Shockable (VF/pVT): Shock 200J biphasic → resume CPR 2 min → reassess.
5
IV/IO access: EJV, antecubital, or IO tibia.
6
Epinephrine 1mg IV/IO every 3-5 min regardless of rhythm.
7
Antiarrhythmic if persistent VF/pVT after 3rd shock:
Amiodarone 300mg IV/IO, second dose 150mg. OR Lidocaine 1-1.5 mg/kg.
8
Advanced airway: SGA or ETT when feasible without interrupting CPR.
9
Search reversible Hs & Ts: Hypoxia, Hypovolemia, H+ acidosis, Hypo/hyperkalemia, Hypothermia. Toxins, Tamponade, Tension pneumothorax, Thrombosis (PE/MI).
10
ROSC: Verify pulse + BP. Capnography >35. Target SpO2 92-98, normocapnia. Cool if appropriate. Transport to PCI-capable center.
Document times: arrest start, first shock, ROSC. Open Code Blue Mode for live timer + drug interval alarms + auto-PCR fields.

Suspected Stroke

Time is brain. Goal: door-to-needle <60min at receiving facility.
1
FAST screen: Face droop, Arm drift, Speech slurred, Time of onset. Cincinnati or NIHSS if trained.
2
Get TIME LAST KNOWN WELL from family/witness — not "time found."
3
Vitals + BGL: Treat hypoglycemia. Avoid aggressive BP lowering unless >220/120.
4
Position: Head of bed 30° if no airway concerns. NPO.
5
Transport rapidly to stroke-capable facility. Pre-notify ED.
6
Establish IV (saline lock or KVO).
7
Document: Last known well, current deficits, BP, BGL, anticoagulant use.

Major Trauma

XABCDE primary survey. Stay & play vs scoop & run by mechanism.
X
Exsanguinating hemorrhage: Tourniquet, direct pressure, wound packing with hemostatic gauze.
A
Airway with C-spine control: Manual stabilization, jaw thrust. Suction. SGA/intubation if GCS <8.
B
Breathing: Inspect, palpate, auscultate. Needle decompression for tension PTX 14G 2nd ICS MCL.
C
Circulation: 2 large-bore IVs. Permissive hypotension SBP 80-90 if no head injury. TXA 1g IV within 3h.
D
Disability: GCS, pupils, BGL, lateralizing signs.
E
Exposure: Look for hidden wounds. Prevent hypothermia.
→
Pre-alert trauma center: Mechanism, vitals, interventions, ETA.

Heat Illness

Major risk during Hajj. Heat stroke is medical emergency.
1
Differentiate:
Heat exhaustion: temp <40°C, sweating, headache, dizzy, intact mental status
Heat stroke: temp ≥40°C, AMS/seizure/coma. EMERGENCY.
2
For heat stroke: Strip clothing, ice packs to neck/groin/axillae, cold water immersion if available, evaporative cooling.
3
Target core temp <39°C: Stop cooling at 39 to prevent overshoot.
4
IV fluids: NS or LR 500ml-1L. Avoid overhydration.
5
Monitor: Core temp, mental status, BP, urine output. Rhabdomyolysis risk.
6
Antipyretics DO NOT WORK for heat stroke. No aspirin/acetaminophen.

Airway Obstruction

Foreign body, anaphylaxis, trauma, or burn.
1
Conscious + effective cough: Encourage cough. Do NOT intervene.
2
Conscious + ineffective: 5 back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich). Alternate until cleared or unconscious.
3
Unconscious: Begin CPR. Each ventilation, check airway for visible foreign body. Remove only if visible.
4
Magill forceps if FB visible on laryngoscopy.
5
Surgical airway (cric) only if cannot oxygenate by any means and trained.

Anaphylaxis

Multi-system involvement + recent exposure. Epi first, always.
1
Epinephrine FIRST 0.5mg IM (adult) anterolateral thigh. Repeat every 5-15min as needed.
2
Position: Supine + legs elevated (unless airway compromise — then sitting).
3
Oxygen high-flow. Suction. Prepare airway.
4
IV access + fluids: NS 1-2L bolus if hypotensive.
5
Adjuncts (after epi): Albuterol nebulized for bronchospasm. Diphenhydramine 25-50mg IV. Methylprednisolone 125mg IV.
6
Refractory shock: Epi infusion 0.1mcg/kg/min if available.

MCI Activation

Mass Casualty Incident — patients exceed available resources.
1
Declare MCI in radio + via /mci page. Include location, mechanism, estimated patient count.
2
Establish command: Incident Commander, Triage Officer, Treatment Officer, Transport Officer. Designate triage area, treatment area (red/yellow/green), staging.
3
START triage: 30 seconds per patient. Walking = GREEN. Then check breathing, perfusion (cap refill), mental status.
4
Apply triage tags: Red (immediate), Yellow (delayed), Green (minor), Black (expectant/deceased). Document in /triage.
5
Notify receiving facilities: Number, severity. Activate hospital MCI plan.
6
Request mutual aid + escalate to SRCA / MoH.

Seizure / Status Epilepticus

Treat after 5min of continuous seizure or 2+ without recovery.
1
Protect from injury: Clear area. Cushion head. Nothing in mouth. Roll to left lateral when possible.
2
Check BGL. Treat hypoglycemia D10 100ml IV.
3
First-line if seizing >5min: Midazolam 10mg IM or 5mg IV/IO. May repeat once after 5 min.
4
Position post-ictal: Recovery position. Oxygen if hypoxic. Suction.
5
Look for cause: Trauma, hyponatremia, hypoglycemia, fever (Hajj fevers — meningitis), eclampsia, drugs/withdrawal.
6
Transport if first seizure, status, or recurrent seizures.

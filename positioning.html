Per-shift coverage timeline

Each row is a shift code. Each column is an hour 00–24. A filled cell means at least one unit on that shift is on duty during that hour — the number inside is how many of the 10 sites it covers. Hours where two shifts overlap (e.g. 05:00 day-shift ramp-up while night is still on; 17:00 night-shift ramp-up while day is still on) are outlined in gold — that's the operational handover, not a staffing surge. This is why the heatmap above shows "peaks" at those hours.
Why does the heatmap above peak at 05:00 and 17:00? Both day and night shifts are physically present during 1-hour handover for continuity of care. The total para count adds both shifts together, which inflates the cell number by ~15–30 paramedics — same staff in different shift slots, not double the workforce. Cells marked with ⇄ in the heatmap above are handover cells.

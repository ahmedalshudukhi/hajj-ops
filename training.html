Training Drills

Pre-built scenarios for quick drill activation. Drills create flagged dispatches that don't affect live metrics.

How drills work

Click "Start Drill" on any scenario to create a flagged training incident (is_drill=1). It appears in /dispatch and /active like a real call so paramedics can practice the workflow — but stays out of /command, /surge, and /sla. Use after the drill to debrief on time targets and expected actions.
